## Pros & Cons

**Non-custodial cards** (EtherFi, Plasma One, Gnosis Pay)  
- ✅ You control funds  
- ✅ Lower exchange risk  
- ⚠️ Availability depends on region

**Cards that stopped in 2026:** Ready (issuer Kulipa wound down, late July), Cypher (acquired by Nium; service ended 2026-09-06), Solflare (paused since 2026-07-28)  
Check the status column before applying.

**Custodial cards** (Coinbase, Gemini)  
- ✅ Simple onboarding  
- ❌ Exchange custody risk
