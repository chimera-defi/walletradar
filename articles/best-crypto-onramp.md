# Best Crypto On-Ramp in 2026

## Quick Answer

- **Highest score:** **CoinGate (90)** and **BitPay (89)**, both aimed at merchant payments with two-way support. **Stripe (88)** is the highest-scoring developer ramp, but its onramp is in public preview and needs an application
- **Best documented EU bank-transfer fees:** **Ramp (86)**, up to 1.40% for manual bank transfer, minimum 2.49 EUR
- **Best Coinbase integration:** **Coinbase Pay (84)**, now branded Coinbase Onramp, 2.5% credit card and 0.5% ACH
- **Card fees to compare:** **MoonPay (79)** up to 4.5%; **Ramp (86)** up to 3.9% for USD/EUR/GBP cards and up to 5.45% for other currencies

Source: `RAMPS.md`, checked 2026-10-17. Scores are generated from the table columns, so a fee or coverage change can move them.
