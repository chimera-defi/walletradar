DETAILS docs now render inside `/docs/<category>/#details`; `/docs/glossary/` redirects to `/glossary/`; 6 thin articles became 2 (`best-ethereum-wallet` sections, `wallet-setup-guide`). Details in `SOFTWARE_WALLETS_DETAILS.md` (Recheck log) and `docs/VERIFICATION.md`.

Wirex (legacy app discontinued for EEA 2026-09-30), Gnosis Pay (cashback ended 2026-09-30, secondary) and Plutus (status to Verify) updated. Payy, Amp Pay, CryptoSpend and Wirex links fixed.
